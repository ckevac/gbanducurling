Welcome

We play at the Whitby Curling Club every Monday from 4:30 to 6:30 pm, October through April. Our members are men from Durham Region and the surrounding area.

Teams are shuffled every four weeks, so you play with almost everyone over the season.

How teams work

Each season has 5 rounds of 4 games. Teams are reshuffled at the start of every round, and every team is known by its skip.

A win is worth 2 points and a tie 1. Every player on the team gets the points and ends won, and the standings are kept by position.

Missing a game?

Let Jeff Sisson know as soon as you can. His number and email are in the weekly league emails.

  1. Text Jeff your name and the date or dates you'll miss.
  2. No cell phone? Email him the same details.
  3. Can't text or email? Call and leave a message.

Jeff lines up spares as absences come in. If a spot can't be filled, players are moved between teams so no team has fewer than 3 and no game is cancelled.
